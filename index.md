# MathNinja Privacy Policy

*Effective September 28, 2026*

MathNinja is a math practice game for children, made by Ariel De Prada. This policy
explains what the app does with information. The short version: **nothing leaves
the device.**

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** MathNinja does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal
information. It has:

- no accounts or sign-in
- no advertising
- no analytics or tracking
- no in-app purchases
- no chat or social features
- no third-party code that collects data
- no internet access: the app never goes online (on Android it doesn't even request
  the internet permission)

## What the app stores on the device

To pick which math facts to practise next, the app keeps a progress file on the
device itself:

- which math facts have been answered, whether each answer was right, and how long it took
- which stages have been cleared, and the best time for each
- the ninja colour picked on the menu, and whether a parent turned on "unlock all stages"

This file never leaves the device. We cannot see it, and it is not linked to a
name, email, device ID or any other identifier.

## Deleting the data

- **Parents screen**: on the menu, hold the Parents button, then tap
  **Reset all progress**.
- **Uninstall**: removing the app deletes the progress file.

## Children's privacy

MathNinja is made for children aged 7 to 10. Because it collects no personal
information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13, in line with
the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles information, we will update this policy
and its effective date before releasing that version.
